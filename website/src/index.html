Good questions

Before you connect.

Will silence disconnect the stream?

No. AudioBridge keeps the session alive even when the source is silent, so a pause in playback is not mistaken for a disconnected device.

Does the audio go through a cloud server?

No. The audio relay runs directly between your devices over the selected local Wi-Fi or USB connection. Internet access is used for update checks and downloads.

Which platforms are supported?

Windows and Android are the primary early-access platforms. An experimental Linux Wi-Fi build also exists with a smaller feature set.

Is AudioBridge already a paid app?

Not yet. Version 1.4.2 is free early access. The roadmap above shows the hardening and store work planned before a paid Pro launch.
